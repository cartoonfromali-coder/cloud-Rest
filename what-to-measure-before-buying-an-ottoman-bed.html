What Should You Measure Before Buying an Ottoman Bed?

Taking the right measurements before ordering an ottoman bed can prevent problems with room layout, access and the lifting mechanism.

Quick answer: Measure the available floor space, the position of surrounding furniture, the complete external bed dimensions and the clearance needed to lift the storage section. Mattress size alone is not enough.

Start with the bedroom itself

Before looking closely at a particular bed, measure the area where it will be positioned. This gives you a realistic idea of which bed sizes can work.

Measure both the width and length available and consider any areas that cannot be occupied because of doors, wardrobes, windows or other furniture.

Measurements that matter

Available width Measure the usable width of the area where the bed will sit.
Available length Check the usable floor length and account for doors or furniture.
External bed size Compare your room measurements with the complete bed dimensions, not just mattress size.
Opening clearance Make sure the lifting section has enough room to operate without hitting nearby obstacles.

Why mattress size is not enough

A mattress size tells you how much sleeping surface you will have, but the bed frame can extend beyond those dimensions.

The headboard, side rails, foot section and other structural elements can all affect the overall footprint.

Always compare the manufacturer's external dimensions with your own room measurements before ordering.

Check the position of nearby furniture

Look at everything around the planned bed position. Wardrobe doors and drawers need to open, while bedside tables should not interfere with access to the bed.

Also consider whether the bed will make it difficult to reach windows, sockets or other frequently used parts of the room.

Doors and wardrobes

Measure the opening path of nearby doors and wardrobe doors. A bed that technically fits may still make the room difficult to use if it blocks an opening.

Bedside furniture

If you plan to keep bedside tables, include their width and position when working out how much floor space remains around the bed.

Do not forget the lifting movement

Ottoman storage is accessed from above, so the bed needs enough room for the upper section to lift.

Check the manufacturer's information about how the particular model opens and consider whether anything positioned nearby could interfere with that movement.

Think about the finished bed height

The final height of the bed depends on both the base and mattress. This can affect how the bed feels within the room and how easy it is to get in and out.

If the room has low furniture, windowsills or other fixed features nearby, consider how the height of the bed will relate to them.

Make a simple floor plan

If you are unsure whether the bed will fit comfortably, mark the proposed footprint on the floor using removable tape.

This gives you a much better sense of walking space than relying on measurements alone. You can also mark bedside tables and other large furniture to test the complete layout.

Final measurement checklist

  • Measure the usable width of the bedroom.
  • Measure the usable length.
  • Check the complete external dimensions of the bed.
  • Measure the position of nearby furniture.
  • Check door and wardrobe opening areas.
  • Allow enough room for the ottoman lifting action.
  • Consider the finished height of the bed and mattress.
  • Use floor tape if you want to visualise the final footprint.

Final thought

Measuring before buying an ottoman bed is about more than checking whether the mattress will fit. The complete frame, surrounding furniture and lifting movement all need to work together.

Taking these measurements in advance makes it much easier to choose a bed that fits the room comfortably and allows the storage to be used as intended.
