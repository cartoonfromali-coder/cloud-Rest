How Do You Access Storage in an Ottoman Bed?

Understanding how an ottoman bed opens can help you choose a design that makes the storage space practical to use every day.

Quick answer: Ottoman bed storage is normally accessed by lifting the mattress and sleeping platform to reveal the space underneath. The lifting system is important because it determines how easily the storage area can be opened and closed.

How does an ottoman bed open?

An ottoman bed uses a lifting mechanism to raise the upper section of the bed and provide access to the storage area beneath it.

Instead of requiring drawers to slide into the room, the storage is accessed from above. This can be particularly useful when floor space around the sides of the bed is limited.

The exact lifting arrangement varies between bed designs, so it is worth checking the mechanism before choosing a particular model.

Why the lifting mechanism matters

The storage may be large, but it is only useful if you can access it comfortably. A well-suited lifting system should make opening and closing the bed manageable for regular use.

Ease of lifting Consider how much effort is needed to raise the sleeping platform.
Opening direction Check which part of the bed lifts and whether the movement suits your room.
Clearance Make sure there is enough surrounding space to use the mechanism properly.
Everyday access Think about whether you will be opening the storage occasionally or regularly.

What can affect how easy it is to lift?

The mattress and bedding sit on the lifting section, so their weight can affect how the mechanism feels in use.

This is why it is useful to check the manufacturer's information about the intended setup rather than assuming every mattress will behave in exactly the same way.

Mattress weight

A heavier mattress adds weight to the lifting platform. If you are replacing a mattress later, check compatibility with the bed's lifting system.

Amount of bedding

Large duvets, blankets and other bedding also add weight. Consider your normal bedding setup when thinking about everyday operation.

Do you need space around the bed?

Although an ottoman bed can save floor space compared with drawer storage, it still needs enough room for the lifting action.

Before positioning the bed, consider walls, wardrobes, bedside furniture and other obstacles that could interfere with the movement of the bed.

How should you organise the storage?

Top-opening storage is particularly useful for items that are not needed several times a day. Spare bedding, seasonal clothing and less frequently used household items can all be easier to keep out of sight.

Using storage bags or separate sections can also make the large compartment easier to organise.

What to check before buying

  • Understand how the lifting mechanism operates.
  • Check which part of the bed lifts.
  • Consider the mattress weight and bedding you normally use.
  • Make sure there is sufficient clearance for the opening movement.
  • Check the complete bed dimensions.
  • Think about how frequently you expect to access the storage.
  • Organise the storage so commonly used items remain easy to reach.

Final thought

The main advantage of an ottoman bed is not simply having storage underneath. The way that storage is accessed is equally important.

By checking the lifting mechanism, surrounding clearance, mattress compatibility and overall dimensions, you can choose an ottoman bed that makes good use of the available bedroom space.
