Is an Ottoman Bed Good for Rooms With Limited Storage?

When wardrobe and cupboard space is limited, using the area beneath the bed can provide another practical place to keep everyday bedroom items.

Quick answer: An ottoman bed can be a useful choice when storage is limited because it uses space beneath the mattress that would otherwise remain unused. The best option depends on the room layout, lifting access and how much storage you actually need.

Why consider storage beneath the bed?

Bedrooms often have a large amount of floor area occupied by the bed, yet the space beneath it is not always used efficiently.

An ottoman design turns this hidden area into accessible storage. This can reduce the need for additional storage furniture and may help keep the visible part of the bedroom less cluttered.

When is an ottoman bed particularly useful?

Small bedrooms Useful when there is little room for additional wardrobes, cabinets or drawers.
Limited cupboards Extra under-bed storage can supplement the storage already available.
Seasonal items The space can be useful for items that are not required every day.
Less visible clutter Keeping spare items beneath the bed can leave more of the room visually clear.

Ottoman storage versus drawers

Both approaches can be useful, but they work differently.

Drawers require space to pull outward from the side or end of the bed. This means furniture or narrow walkways can make them difficult to use.

Ottoman storage is accessed by lifting the upper section of the bed instead. This can be advantageous when there is limited side clearance, although the room still needs enough space for the lifting movement.

Think about what you want to store

Not every item needs to be stored under the bed. An ottoman bed is particularly useful for larger or less frequently used bedroom items.

  • Spare bedding and sheets
  • Seasonal blankets
  • Occasionally used clothing
  • Storage bags and organisers
  • Other suitable bedroom items

Keeping frequently used items towards the most accessible areas can make a large storage compartment easier to manage.

Check the room before choosing

An ottoman bed can save storage space, but the bed itself still needs to fit comfortably within the room.

Measure the complete footprint and consider the position of wardrobes, doors, windows and bedside furniture. Also check the space needed for the lifting mechanism.

Do not measure only the mattress

The mattress dimensions do not always represent the complete external size of the bed. Always check the manufacturer's full dimensions before buying.

Consider how often you will use the storage

If you plan to open the storage every week, ease of lifting becomes particularly important. If the storage is mainly for seasonal items, occasional access may be enough.

Your usage pattern should therefore influence the type of ottoman bed you choose.

Questions to ask before buying

  • How much storage do you actually need?
  • Will the bed fit comfortably in the available floor space?
  • Is there enough clearance for the lifting mechanism?
  • How frequently will you access the storage?
  • Is the mattress suitable for the lifting system?
  • Will the bed reduce the need for additional storage furniture?

Final thought

When bedroom storage is limited, an ottoman bed can make better use of space that is already occupied by the bed. It can be especially useful where there is not enough room for large drawers or additional storage furniture.

The key is to choose a size and lifting arrangement that work with the room rather than treating storage capacity as the only consideration.
