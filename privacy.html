Website Privacy

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Cloud & Rest handles information associated with visits to our website and interactions with our content.

1. Scope of This Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to information collected through the Cloud & Rest website. It covers ordinary website browsing, technical information, analytics, cookies and information voluntarily submitted through available contact features.

Product links may take visitors to independent retailer websites. Those external destinations are not covered by this policy.

2. Browsing the Website

Most pages can be viewed without creating an account. Like other websites, our hosting and technical systems may process information associated with a browser or device connecting to the site.

  • IP address or approximate network information.
  • Browser and operating-system information.
  • Device and screen characteristics.
  • Pages requested and general interaction data.
  • Referring or entry pages.

3. Information You Choose to Send

If you contact Cloud & Rest, we may receive the information included in your message, together with details such as your name or email address if you choose to provide them.

Please provide only information needed for the purpose of your enquiry.

4. What We Do With Information

Collected information may support the operation and improvement of the website. Examples include:

  • Maintaining website functionality.
  • Analysing general visitor behaviour.
  • Improving articles and product guides.
  • Responding to enquiries.
  • Maintaining website security.
  • Understanding referral and affiliate performance.

5. Cookies and Measurement

Cookies and related technologies may be used for basic functionality, preferences, analytics and measurement.

Third-party services may use their own technologies when their features are present. Browser settings generally provide controls for managing cookies.

6. Affiliate Links

Cloud & Rest may receive compensation from certain external product links. Affiliate networks or retailers may use referral information and cookies to identify the source of a visit.

Clicking an affiliate link does not transfer ownership of your personal information to Cloud & Rest. Activity on the external retailer's website is subject to that retailer's own privacy policy.

7. External Platforms

Our website may link to retailers, affiliate networks, analytics providers and other third-party services. Their collection and processing practices are outside our control.

Before submitting information to an external service, visitors should read the privacy information provided by that service.

8. Disclosure of Information

Information may be processed by service providers supporting hosting, analytics, security and technical operation.

Disclosure may also occur where required by law, necessary to respond to lawful requests, or reasonably required to protect the website and its users.

9. Security and Retention

Reasonable safeguards are used where appropriate, but no online system can promise absolute security.

Information is retained only for periods reasonably connected with its purpose, subject to technical and third-party service retention policies.

10. Privacy Requests

Where applicable law gives you privacy rights, you may request access to, correction of, deletion of or other control over certain personal information.

Requests can be submitted through an available website contact method. Applicable legal exceptions may affect how a request is handled.

11. Children

We do not intentionally use this website to request personal information from children. If unnecessary information concerning a child has been submitted, please contact us through the website.

12. Updates to This Policy

The Privacy Policy may be changed when our website, technologies, services or applicable requirements change. The current version will remain available on this page.
